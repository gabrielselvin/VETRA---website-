Ventures

Independent products, one ecosystem.

Each VETRA venture solves one specific problem for one specific group of people. Some will grow into their own companies — that's the point.

Venture 01 — Active

SkillLens

A skill-assessment and career-direction platform built for students who don't know what they're actually good at yet. SkillLens turns scattered self-doubt into a clear, evidence-based picture of strengths — and a direction to point them in.

How we build it

Problem

Students choose subjects and careers with little real insight into their own skills.

Approach

Structured assessments translate raw ability into a legible skill profile.

Outcome

Clearer, evidence-backed direction for subjects, careers, and next steps.

Problem

Useful items sit unused while people nearby need them for a short time only.

Approach

A simple marketplace to rent items out, instead of buying or letting them sit idle.

Outcome

Lower cost for people who need things, less waste for people who own them.

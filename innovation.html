Innovation Lab

Where unproven ideas get tested honestly.

Before an idea becomes a venture, it comes through the lab — tested quickly, judged honestly, and either advanced or set aside.

Not every idea deserves a company

The Innovation Lab is where VETRA tries things without committing to them — small prototypes, applied AI experiments, and early product sketches, built just far enough to know if a real problem is really being solved.

Ideas that hold up move on to become full ventures. The rest still teach us something, and that's considered a fair outcome.

Current focus

Applied AI for skill assessment, and lightweight matching systems for marketplaces.

Cycle length

Short, deliberately — a few weeks per experiment, not months.

Bar to graduate

A real person, outside the team, finds it genuinely useful.

What we experiment with
